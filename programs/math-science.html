What We Cover

Courses & Subjects

Elementary School

Basic Math

Number sense, addition, subtraction, multiplication, division fundamentals and fractions.

Intro to Science

Introduction to life science, earth science, and physical science concepts.

Middle School

Pre-Algebra

Variables, expressions, equations, and the bridge into formal algebra.

Algebra I

Linear equations, inequalities, functions, and graphing.

Earth Science

Regents-aligned coverage of geology, meteorology, and astronomy.

Life Science / Biology

Cells, ecosystems, genetics, and the foundations of living systems.

High School

Algebra II

Polynomials, rational functions, exponential and logarithmic equations.

Geometry

Proofs, congruence, similarity, coordinate geometry, and trigonometry basics.

Pre-Calculus

Trigonometry, vectors, polar coordinates, and limits.

Statistics

Data analysis, probability, distributions, and statistical reasoning.

Biology

Regents and AP-level biology: cells, genetics, evolution, and ecology.

Chemistry

Atomic structure, bonding, reactions, stoichiometry, and Regents prep.

Physics

Mechanics, waves, electricity, and magnetism — Regents through AP.

College Level

Calculus I & II & III

Limits, derivatives, integrals, and series — aligned to your college syllabus.

College Physics

Topics such as mechanics, heat, electricity, magnetism and modern physics.

College Algebra

Functions, matrices, complex numbers, and advanced algebraic concepts.
