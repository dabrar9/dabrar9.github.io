Not Sure Which to Take?

SAT vs. ACT — a quick comparison

SAT
  • 2 sections: Math + Reading & Writing
  • Scored out of 1600
  • Digital format (as of 2024)
  • Slightly more reading-heavy math
  • No science section
ACT
  • 4 sections: English, Math, Reading, Science
  • Scored out of 36
  • Paper and pencil format
  • Faster pace, more questions
  • Includes a Science reasoning section
