Free Resources

Extra Resources

A curated list of free, high-quality websites to help NYC students study smarter — covering math, science, history, test prep, and more.

100% Free to Use
Math

Math Tools & Practice

Regents

JMAP

Every NY Regents math exam ever written, organized by topic. Includes answer keys, worksheets, and teacher resources for Algebra I, II, and Geometry.

jmap.org All Levels

Khan Academy

Free video lessons and practice problems for every math topic from basic arithmetic through AP Calculus and Statistics. Also syncs with SAT prep.

khanacademy.org Tool

Desmos

A powerful free graphing calculator used on NY Regents and SAT exams. Practice using it now so it's familiar come test day.

desmos.com Tool

Wolfram Alpha

Step-by-step math solver for algebra, calculus, statistics, and more. Great for checking your work and understanding how answers are reached.

wolframalpha.com Tool

Mathway

An advanced calculator that allows you to decide what type of operation you would like to do with a function. Provides step-by-step answers.

mathway.com
Science

Science & Lab Resources

All Levels

CK-12

Free textbooks, simulations, and practice for Biology, Chemistry, Physics, and Earth Science. Fully aligned to NY state standards.

ck12.org Simulations

PhET Simulations

Interactive science and math simulations from the University of Colorado. Perfect for visualizing concepts like circuits, forces, waves, and chemical reactions.

phet.colorado.edu Video

Bozeman Science

High-quality science videos covering AP Biology, AP Chemistry, AP Physics, and AP Environmental Science. Clear, concise, and exam-focused.

bozemanscience.com
English & History

Reading, Writing & History

ELA

CommonLit

Free reading passages with guided questions for grades 3–12. Includes fiction, nonfiction, and poetry — great for building comprehension and analysis skills.

commonlit.org Writing

Purdue OWL

The gold standard for writing guides. Covers essay structure, MLA and APA citation, grammar rules, and research paper formatting.

purdue.edu/owl Regents

Varsity Tutors — English Regents

Free English Regents practice tests with detailed answer explanations. Good for building familiarity with the text analysis and argument essay formats.

varsitytutors.com History

History Sage

Detailed outlines and essay guides for AP US History and AP World History. Helpful for DBQ and LEQ practice.

historysage.com
Test Prep

SAT, ACT & Regents Prep

SAT

Khan Academy SAT Prep

Official College Board partner for SAT prep. Personalized practice linked to your PSAT results. Completely free and proven to raise scores.

khanacademy.org/SAT Regents

NYSED Regents Exams

The official source for all NY Regents exams. Download past exams and scoring guides directly from the NY State Education Department.

nysedregents.org SAT / ACT

CrackSAT

A large library of real SAT and ACT practice tests available for free. One of the best places to find official past exams to practice with.

cracksat.net
NYC Students

NYC-Specific Resources

Library

New York Public Library

Free access to databases, e-books, tutoring, and study spaces across all five boroughs. Your library card unlocks a huge amount of free digital resources.

nypl.org NYC DOE

NYC Schools Portal

The official NYC Department of Education site. Access grades, attendance, school resources, and information about programs and opportunities.

schools.nyc.gov Free Internet

NYC Free Internet (iGetIt)

NYC's program providing free or low-cost broadband to qualifying households. Don't let a lack of internet access get in the way of studying.

igetit.nyc
